My World

Privacy

What My World keeps about you, and what it does with it.

What is kept

Your account: your email address, your name and picture as the way you sign in gives them (Google or GitHub), and how you sign in. Your data: what you add to My World or import into it (books, concerts, museums, courses, audio books, podcasts, YouTube videos, games, certifications, people, places and the rest). It is kept in a database on Google Cloud and is yours: no other account can see it.

YouTube

If you connect your YouTube account on the Imports page, My World asks Google for read-only access to it (the youtube.readonly scope) and keeps the token Google gives, encrypted. It is used only when you run an importer, to read the playlists you choose and the details of their videos (title, channel, category, length, upload day, views), which are added to your own YouTube videos in My World. Nothing is ever written to your YouTube account. Disconnecting on the Imports page revokes the token at Google and deletes it; so does revoking My World's access in your Google account's settings.

Audible

If you connect your Audible account on the Imports page, you sign in at Amazon yourself, on Amazon's own page: My World never sees your password. Amazon then adds My World to your account's devices, and My World keeps that device's keys, encrypted. They are used only when you run the Audible library importer, to read the books of your library (title, authors, narrators, series, genres, length, how much you heard, the rating at Audible, the cover), which are added to your own audio books in My World. Nothing is ever written to your Audible account. Disconnecting on the Imports page removes the device from your account and deletes the keys; so does removing the device in your Amazon account.

My World's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What is not done with it

Nothing you keep in My World is sold, shared with anyone, used for advertising, or used to train any model. It is used only to show it back to you.

Deleting it

You can delete anything you added from within the app. To have your whole account and everything in it deleted, write to the address below.
